# The Black Universe

An evolving browser science-fiction game: explore the starship **Erebus**, restore its power network, awaken a colossal **universe forge**, then pilot **Kestrel** through space and land on **Nysa**.

**Play:** https://the-black-universe-erebus.harshit-0207.chatgpt.site

This is a playable foundation toward an ambitious universe simulation. It does not yet provide television-level photorealism, planet-wide exploration, multiplayer, or general-purpose AI NPCs.

## Run

Node.js 22+: `npm ci`, then `npm run dev`. Open the printed URL. `npm test` verifies progression/save invariants; `npm run build` checks TypeScript and produces static `dist/`. `npm run preview` serves that build. With the dev server running, `npm run test:browser` exercises the mission and collision flow in Chromium (first install it with `npx playwright install chromium`).

## Expedition 001

**Clear view:** information panels start hidden. Click **INFO** / press **H** to show or hide them. Click **1P / 3P** / press **C** to switch first/third person for both walking and flying. Touch movement, E, Brake/Boost/Target and pause remain usable with panels hidden. Preferences persist locally. See [camera and HUD scope](docs/VIEWS.md).

Enter the Erebus. Move with WASD/arrows, look with the mouse, sprint with Shift, jump with Space, and interact with E. Solid collision geometry blocks interaction through walls; approach an unobstructed console or officer. Escape pauses. Touch devices have a left movement joystick, right-side drag camera, and an E action button. Open menu settings for performance mode, audio and mission reset. The navigation card shows heading, direction and distance to the next mission waypoint, routing room crossings through bulkheads. See [navigation controls and scope](docs/NAVIGATION.md).

1. Walk to the forward bridge. Speak to Officer Vega and activate the navigation relay. Officer conversations now offer **Next objective**, **Survey debrief** and **Ship systems** topics, reporting your saved scans, actual routing and remaining coolant.
2. Use the access panel beside the port bulkhead to open engineering. Activate the power relay near the reactor. Engineering has an interior access panel for returning to the concourse.
3. Open the starboard bulkhead and activate the observatory relay. Its interior panel controls the same bulkhead.
4. Return to the central bridge console and initialize the architect. A new world, Nysa, appears outside.
5. Scan it from the bridge and launch Kestrel (the concourse shuttle-bay console also launches it). W/S controls throttle, A/D turns, mouse/drag or Up/Down aims, Shift boosts and Space brakes. Follow Nysa's approach beacons; **BRAKE NOW** estimates stopping distance, and the amber **VELOCITY** diamond shows actual travel direction. Brake below 18 m/s within 100 m, choose **PAD / L** for Survey or Arch pad while flying, then press E for guided touchdown. Arch pad lands near the resonance survey site; selection locks during capture. Braking takes priority over held thrust/boost. In third person, Kestrel banks into turns and its engine plumes respond to throttle/boost, cut during braking and point down during guided lift.
6. Watch the radar-guided cockpit descent over Nysa, then explore on foot and sample the luminous outcrop. Open **FIELD LOG** with **J** or its button to view a north-up terrain map and track optional scans at the resonance arch and ridge weather station. Tap the map or enter local X/Z coordinates to place a temporary survey waypoint; INFO shows its direction/distance. Arriving restores mission guidance. Board the parked shuttle at its aft hatch/beacon, watch the ascent, fly back to Erebus's front bay beacons, brake and dock with E. Guided docking aligns through the open hangar; nearby side/rear approaches are rejected. See [flight controls and scope](docs/FLIGHT.md).

Engineering also has an optional **auxiliary coolant reserve**: interact with the marked cabinet, then discharge either of two cartridges to remove 25 thermal-load points each. A trip still needs a manual reset. After landing, **J / FIELD LOG → Track cache** guides you to a one-time Nysa refill in a textured CGTrader cargo crate on a terrain-fitted pallet; samples and scans remain separate. Normal Cooling is always available.

Engineering also has a containment console: choose Balanced for stable operation, Genesis for a stronger forge bus at the cost of heat, or Cooling to recover an overload. If containment trips, cool to 40% and reset. See [power model and controls](docs/POWER.md).

Progress saves locally on this browser/device; corrupt or unavailable storage fails safely. Reset is in the menu. Kestrel offers bounded manual spaceflight with a cockpit, assisted inertia, boost/braking, collision recovery and guided final landing/docking. Touch uses the movement joystick, right-side aiming and held BRAKE/BOOST buttons; T or TARGET switches destination in flight. Nysa offers one bounded surface excursion; reloading starts safely aboard with collected samples retained. Nysa uses slope-colored, textured ground; High mode adds fine normal and roughness detail. Its orbital view now has coastlines, polar ice and drifting clouds. See [surface controls and scope](docs/EXCURSION.md).

## Technology and scope

TypeScript + Three.js/WebGL 2 + Rapier WASM + Vite. Fixed-step kinematic capsule movement with floor/wall/door collision, anti-crush bulkheads, procedural metallic surfaces, real lights/shadows, restrained bloom, animated forge rings and local synthesized ambience. Bundled local character assets; no runtime keys, database or AI service. Typography uses local system fonts; no external runtime requests are required. Vega and Ion now use the same locally bundled, skinned Mixamo Ely character with captured idle/walking clips, state-aware scripted dialogue, physical local patrols, collision-based visitor awareness, proximity attention, and an engineering containment-watch duty. Procedural androids remain a loading-failure fallback. Ion routes to diagnostics during a trip and returns to patrol after reset.

Desktop modern Chrome/Firefox/Edge with hardware acceleration is recommended. Touch controls are available; actual mobile GPU performance still needs device testing. High mode caps rendering resolution at 1.75×; performance mode uses 1×, no shadow map/bloom and four smoothly selected nearby point lights instead of all nine. Distant room spill can differ; High keeps the full lighting rig. Startup downloads include the physics WASM bundle (~1 MB compressed initial JS) and an asynchronous ~3.3 MB character GLB shared by both officers. Lossless WebP textures reduce that asset by 31.6%; a fingerprinted model URL supports browser caching across updates. Movement catches up with bounded 60 Hz physics steps at lower render rates; this corrects simulation slowdown without increasing GPU FPS. See [frame-pacing evidence and limits](docs/FRAME-PACING.md). Static ship boxes now use spatial instancing to reduce draw calls while keeping dynamic doors/screens separate; see [rendering measurements and limits](docs/RENDERING.md). The parked craft now loads aboard at startup (about 2.57 MB encoded), sharing geometry/maps with its flight instances. Exterior station downloads begin at launch. If one fails, its fallback stays playable and the next launch/boarding retries it; successful models stay shared and are not downloaded again. Further streaming/LOD and asset-budget work is on the roadmap.

See [local crew behavior](docs/CREW.md) for the implemented patrol model and its limits.
