# Clear view and third person

Information panels start hidden on a new browser. **INFO** (or H) shows/hides mission, power, navigation, telemetry, contextual prompts, notifications and location together. The pause button, movement joystick, interaction button and flight Brake/Boost/Target buttons remain accessible. Deliberately opened conversations, power controls and field logs remain visible so their controls can be used. J still opens the field log with panels hidden.

**1P / 3P** (or C) switches the walking and flight camera. These two browser-local preferences are independent of mission saves and survive reload; mission reset does not reset them. Storage failures leave session preferences usable. Keyboard shortcuts are available during active exploration, not inside a dialogue/settings form.

Walking third person uses the selected Cyber Biker with captured Mixamo Walking/Running/Jump clips; the officers retain their shared Ely skin. Gait follows actual Rapier displacement and physical jump phase. Bike-only hand contact is separate from on-foot animation. The small procedural fallback remains if loading fails. A .22-meter sphere sweep retracts the shoulder camera against physical walls/doors/terrain; the avatar disappears below .8 meters of camera clearance to avoid filling the screen. Interaction distance and sight are measured from the character's eye with the existing aim direction in either view; the offset camera does not grant extra reach or see-through-wall interaction.

Flight third person displays the selected CGTrader craft behind a chase camera; first person uses the selected authored cockpit. Orbital camera clearance uses the hangar/habitat envelopes and planet sphere. Guided surface flight keeps the craft level. Surface flight, mounted bike/rover and local runway guidance sweep a .22-meter sphere against physical terrain, props and doorways. This catches grazing edges that a center ray misses; the player and own craft/hatch proxies are excluded. Coincident endpoints return zero, and an overlapping start retracts rather than sweeping through a wall. The camera returns to its full distance when the obstruction clears. During surface/orbital flight, extremely close chase contact hides the flying craft rather than showing its interior. Vehicle collision remains the existing conservative assisted-flight model, not detailed rigid-body wing contact. The guided descent/ascent and complete launch/land/walk/board/dock loop are preserved.

The parked craft's 4.94×2.84×6.62-meter collision envelope covers the imported wings/body. Its aft hatch and navigation anchor sit 3.5 meters behind the craft center, outside the hull, so boarding remains reachable from the rear and blocked through the ship. This is a conservative box, not per-triangle contact.

The walkable station uses the Intrepid interior and connected corridor/bedroom with authored triangle collision and functional game doors. Exterior hollow-looking sections retain conservative box collision envelopes; local and orbital representations remain staged. See ASSETS.md for source and optimization evidence.
