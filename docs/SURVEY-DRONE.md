# ECHO survey companion

Complete Silent Network (repair both receivers, activate the communications uplink), then find ECHO beside the outpost. J / FIELD LOG → Locate ECHO provides direction and distance. Approach and press E to recover it; choose Follow me, Hold here or Scan nearby survey site. The command panel closes after a choice and adds no permanent screen clutter.

ECHO follows your walked route on foot, stopping about2.6m behind along that route. Four source-animated propellers turn while its local stabilization holds altitude, including before pairing. Movement and animation freeze while paused, in dialogue or away from Nysa. Driving a bike/rover makes it hold; it does not chase moving vehicles. The field log can locate it again. Completing recovery persists locally; on each new landing the recovered unit deploys beside the selected Kestrel pad in Hold mode. Reload starts aboard, retaining recovery and completed readings. Reset clears them.

Scan records an existing unread arch/weather reading within8m and clear sight after two active seconds. It holds while scanning; choose Follow again afterward. Range and occlusion are checked again before awarding a reading. It cannot scan through walls, reveal distant sites or replace the sample objective.

## Bounds and verification

This is a bounded scripted companion, not a general autonomous agent. A maximum256-point trail records walked corners; a swept56cm-radius sphere prevents passing through solid geometry. It adds no collider that could block walking. Tight/low spaces, disconnected paths or prolonged obstruction stop it; it never warps through a wall. Abrupt separation cancels Follow. Ground rays track the actual supporting deck/terrain, excluding the player. Terrain following is kinematic, not force-based flight or rotor aerodynamics.

The derivative costs97,083 encoded bytes /224,360 decoded GLB bytes,7,302 unique geometry triangles and15,366 rendered triangles over8 mesh instances, with four shared rotor meshes. It loads only on entering Nysa; failed imports retain a simple geometric fallback and retry on explicit re-entry. It adds no image maps, lights, shadow maps or collision handles. Following uses one ground ray plus one sphere sweep per active fixed step (up to60Hz); held units avoid those queries. Shadows can participate in the existing High-mode shadow pass. Physical-device FPS/memory and hosted rendering remain unmeasured.

Meaningful tests cover right-angle routes, bounded history, held/blocked/separated behavior, real Rapier wall sweeps, actual packaged model/materials/rotor motion and bounds, retry behavior, pause, scan timing/visibility, and save compatibility.
