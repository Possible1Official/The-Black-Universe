# Vesper frozen-basin expedition

Recover Wayfinder **Archive05**, then choose **J / FIELD LOG → Track Vesper landing**. Fly Kestrel to Vesper's near-side approach ring at sector **710/130/−1090**, within100m and below18m/s, then press **E**. **T** cancels tracking and restores ordinary Nysa/Erebus guidance. The frozen globe has its own visible physical safety hull.

Three seconds of orbital capture lead into seven seconds of guided descent. Land beside the parked jet in a new96×96m survey basin. Walk, run and jump with existing controls and captured Mixamo clips; INFO remains optional, C switches first/third person. Follow guidance to the western **frost shelf** and northeastern **warm fissure**. Approach each sampler and press E. Both readings unlock **Archive06** at the northern recorder platform. The field log and Vega's survey debrief reflect your findings.

You can leave at any time: approach Kestrel's aft hatch on the southern apron and press E. Seven-second ascent and three-second departure restore manual flight at the same orbital endpoint and13⅓m/s outward speed, targeting Erebus. You can then pilot to Erebus or switch back to Nysa normally. Vesper is selected through the field log rather than adding another item to T's existing two-target cycle.

The **Turbo Bike** and **Sci-fi Rover** park beside the southern apron at X7/Z29 and X−8/Z29. Approach, aim and press E to ride. Left slider sets speed; right joystick steers. W/S adjust speed, A/D steer, and camera look remains independent. Brake/Space overrides acceleration; stop below.6m/s and press E to dismount onto clear nearby ground. Dismount before scanning or boarding Kestrel. C switches views; the bike keeps the textured seated body and hand-to-grip contact in first person, hiding only head parts around the eye camera. The rover uses its enclosed driving view without an exposed rider.

Both vehicles reuse Nysa's original reviewed CGTrader geometry/maps and captured Riding pose. The rover's complete arms pivot at their hips, wheels spin and front knuckles steer; the bike banks and its turbine pod uses damped shocks. These are terrain-fitted visual suspension systems over collision-resolved kinematic proxies, not force-based wheel dynamics. Rover cruise is14m/s, or16m/s after Nysa's delivered recorder contract; bike cruise is12m/s. Cargo stays on Nysa. Parking resets on re-entry, while source loads and collider allocations are reused. Pause clears vehicle demand and stops motion; walking and normal camera clipping restore after dismount/exit.

Recover **ECHO** beside Nysa's communications outpost to bring it on future Vesper visits. It waits beside the southern apron. Approach, aim and press E for **Follow me**, **Hold here** or **Scan nearby survey site**. Follow traces your walked route using swept collision checks and bounded local detours; tight/blocked routes or large separation make it hold safely. It never teleports through obstacles and adds no player-blocking collider. Vehicles cancel following/scanning; dismount and approach ECHO to command it again.

Scan needs an unread frost/warmth sampler within8m of ECHO and clear sight for two active seconds. Range/sight loss cancels the scan; pause/dialogue freeze it. The resulting reading saves exactly like a manual sampler scan. Both distinct readings still gate Archive06. J/FIELD LOG offers **Locate ECHO** and **Track survey objective** without a permanent extra overlay. Its original CGTrader metallic materials and rotor loop are retained; this source has no image maps. Re-entry starts holding beside the apron; reload safely aboard preserves recovered ECHO and readings. Nysa's companion state/world stay separate.

Readings/archive save additively with Nysa missions, cargo and ECHO. Reload starts safely aboard and retains findings. Re-entry reuses the same world/resources. Reset clears the survey and exits to Erebus. Pause/dialogue freeze walking and guidance. Interactions require proximity, aim and clear sight; Archive05 gates entry and two distinct readings gate Archive06.

Vesper has a separate Rapier world and scene; its terrain cannot alter Nysa's collision mesh, props or vehicle tuning. Rendered/collision terrain share a96² grid. Prepared apron, rim rails and equipment have visible matching proxies. Scanned rocks use the reviewed CGTrader mesh/coarse hull with multiple buried supports. The distant crater rim is scenery outside the physical perimeter. Shuttle/recorder source geometry and PBR maps are shared unchanged; these static sources have no animation. Existing character motion is reused.

Scope: one bounded basin supporting walking, two ground vehicles and a local ECHO companion, not global streaming, a simulated biosphere, force-based orbital mechanics or Vesper cargo missions. Ground, sky and globe artwork are procedural, with grain/bump, frost/basalt variation, soft ground contact and distant crater walls. No photorealism, general AI or physical-device GPU/FPS/memory claim.
