# Nysa vehicles and authored interiors

The player uses the user-selected Cyber Biker. Ely remains the two scripted officers. Walking is 1.8 m/s and sprinting (Shift) is 4.2 m/s. Idle/Walking reuse the previously acquired Mixamo motion, retargeted to the compatible 67-joint rig. Running and the seated Riding pose are locally authored, not new Mixamo downloads. Walk/run phase follows actual collision-resolved distance. There is no foot/hand IK, facial animation, suspension simulation or jump clip.

Nysa has one 256×256-meter survey sector with shared render/physics geometry: dunes, basalt mesas, a shallow wetland basin and highland ridges. Terrain colors blend continuously; the wetland has a clipped water surface and instanced reeds. Both pads, sample, scanners, cache and field map remain. There is no global terrain streaming, swimming or another playable planet.

Approach the Turbo Bike or Sci-fi Rover near the selected pad and press E. The left vertical slider requests speed; the right joystick steers. W/S adjust the same speed request, A/D steer, and Space/BRAKE stops. Brake below 1 m/s before E dismounts; collision checks choose a clear nearby place. Third-person mouse/touch look changes the view without changing vehicle heading. The rover's driver is concealed inside its closed cab; its four tire pivots spin with actual travel. Bike wheels remain static. Both use bounded Rapier character-controller proxies, gravity, terrain snap, step/slope limits and solid obstacle collisions, rather than rigid-body suspension/tire friction. The vehicle class can be reused by future planet sectors; currently it runs only on Nysa.

The spacecraft uses OK3D's Sci Fi Flying Ship, normalized into the existing conservative flight envelope. Weapons are decorative. First person shows ViitorX's authored cockpit with glazing removed for a clear world view; decorative instruments do not implement ship systems. Cockpit orientation follows the aircraft while head look stays independent. Third person shows the hull with an independent chase view. Orbital assisted flight and guided atmosphere/docking transitions remain separate representations.

The station uses the visible Intrepid assembly at uniform scale 1.5. Functional relays, diagnostics, crew and door controls are relocated to its rooms. Static collision comes from the exact exported mesh; its original side door panels are replaced by moving game bulkheads. The aft corridor leads to the adapted CGTrader runway, then to the crew bedroom. The orbital hangar also renders this runway during launch/capture, with increased vertical clearance. Local walking rooms and the orbital hangar remain separate staged scenes; walking directly into the orbital scene is not implemented.

All stock sources and account state remain in ignored private `.local/`. Eight optimized derivatives ship as fingerprinted EBU1 resources. The format has a public client decoder/key: it deters standalone stock downloads but is not unextractable DRM. Creator/license/hash/geometry/transfer metadata is published in `public/assets/requested/provenance.json`. Browser and hardware verification limits are recorded in PROGRESS.

Repair follow-up: the biker now uses explicitly lit PBR textures with no skin/clothing emission and two-sided alpha-tested hair. Idle/Walking match world-aligned posed bone directions across the different rest poses. Bike length is 2.5 m (previously 3.6 m), with matching reduced physics envelope and rider seat. Walking uses a 48 cm shoulder capsule, seam-aware authored collision and 32 cm stepping. The launch lounge is widened to approximately 7 m and its low floor trim is removed.

The cabin now contains seemlyhasan's user-selected Cute Girl (#2712319), retaining her original static lying pose, with only rigid normalization/placement and a user-requested 12% larger instance. This is not a rigged or newly animated person. Only the bedroom rear window reveals the locally generated space panorama; the bow retains the actual simulated space/forge view. The panorama is scenic artwork, not a new explorable planet. Corridor/cabin lighting and reduced Nysa distance fog improve visibility.
